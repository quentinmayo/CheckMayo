# Developer support

Buy Me a Coffee is the selected donation provider. The creator profile is pending account verification and payout onboarding. There is no activated donation URL in this release.

The maintainer will complete creator signup at https://buymeacoffee.com/signup, verify the requested email, and configure a payout method. Once the profile is activated, add its verified public URL to `.github/FUNDING.yml` under `custom` and to the hosted registry's `CHECKMAYO_DONATION_URL`.

Do not put the signup password, verification messages, payout details, or personal financial data in this repository.

Commercial support and additional managed security tooling: https://mayoaspm.com/.
